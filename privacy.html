Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. How Peer Umais Spiritual Healing handles the details you share.

Who we are

Peer Umais Spiritual Healing provides spiritual healing sessions and consultation from Birmingham, UK, for visitors in the UK and around the world. This policy explains what information we handle when you use this website or send an inquiry.

What this website collects

This website does not use accounts, take payments, or run advertising or analytics tools. It does not set tracking cookies.

If you use the inquiry form, the website only prepares a message from what you type: your name, email address, WhatsApp or phone number, and the service you choose. The form does not save or send this information to our servers.

How your inquiry reaches us

Pressing Inquire Now opens WhatsApp with your details already written. The inquiry is delivered only when you press Send in WhatsApp. Once sent, your message and number are handled by WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy, as well as by us.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and arrange a consultation session.
  • To provide the session you have booked.
  • To keep a record of our conversation where needed to follow up with you.

We do not sell your information or use it for marketing you have not asked for.

Sensitive information

Sessions can involve personal, spiritual and health-related matters. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. We treat what you tell us as confidential and do not pass it to others unless you ask us to, or the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep conversations only as long as needed to help you and to follow up. You can ask us to delete your conversation at any time.

Third-party links

This website links to WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. Those services are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for how they handle your information.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent at any time. If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Contact

To ask about your information, message us: +44 7735 775484 on WhatsApp.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
