Spiritual Guidance

Wazifa

When and how to perform the Wazifa, with the recitations to read. Guidance provided by Peer Umais.

WHEN / HOW

WHEN

After Magrib

HOW

After Namaz-e-Magrib recite:

  1. Ayat-ul-Karima313 times
  2. Allah (Ism-e-Zaat)66 times
  3. Astagfar313 times

DUA AND DUM

Following are the completion of the Wazifa. You can make Dua for yourself or anyone else for any good purpose. It is also allowed to blow upon (insufflate) water after completion of this Wazifa and drink it, or give the water to some patient to drink. Inshallah any illness will subside.

DURATION

The wazifa has to be done under 40 days and the performer can repeat it as many times he can.

WARNING

The performer must first consult Peer Umais before reciting or performing the Wazifa. Any action taken without the guidance or supervision of Peer Umais is undertaken at the performer’s own responsibility.

313 times

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about services, booking and what happens next.

What services are available?

Peer Umais offers eight services: Spiritual Healing, Protection From Jinn, Evil Eye Protection, Black Magic Removal, Jinn Problems Solution, Online Istikhara, Spiritual Protection, Spiritual Blockage Removal.

How does a session work?

Sessions are arranged directly with Peer Umais after you send an inquiry. How your session will be held is agreed with you at that point.

How can I book?

Choose a service, enter your name, email address and WhatsApp number in the booking form, then select Inquire Now. WhatsApp opens with your details ready to send to Peer Umais. Your inquiry is delivered when you press Send.

Is the consultation online?

Online Istikhara is offered online. For the other services, Peer Umais will confirm with you how your session will take place when replying to your inquiry.

How do I contact Peer Umais?

Use the booking form on this page, or message Peer Umais on WhatsApp at +44 7735 775484. Peer Umais will reply using the email address or WhatsApp number you provide.

What information do I need to provide?

Your name, email address, WhatsApp number, and the service you would like. Nothing else is required to send an inquiry.

How does pricing and payment work?

The fee shown on this website is £30. This website does not take payment. Use the fees button in the Sessions section to ask Peer Umais on WhatsApp how the fee applies to your sessions.

What happens after I send an inquiry?

Once you press Send in WhatsApp, Peer Umais reads your inquiry and replies using the email address or WhatsApp number you gave, to arrange your session. Sending an inquiry does not confirm a session by itself.
